# Forge Tab privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Forge Tab is a Chrome new tab page made by Forge Stack Partners LLC.

## What Forge Tab stores

Your shortcuts, groups, notes, saved commands, status checks and settings are stored with Chrome's storage on your own computer. They are never sent to Forge Stack Partners or anyone else. Uninstalling Forge Tab deletes them. Use **Settings → Export layout** first if you want to keep a copy.

## What Forge Tab collects

Nothing. Forge Tab has no account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no servers of its own. It does not read your browsing history or the pages you visit.

## When Forge Tab contacts other websites

Only when you ask it to:

- **Get logo:** fetches the web page and icon files of the site you type in, to make a shortcut icon. If the site has no usable icon, it asks Google's public favicon service for that site's icon.
- **Status checks:** sends a simple request to each address you add in Settings, about once a minute while a Forge Tab page is open, to show whether it is up. For an Ollama check it also reads that server's list of models.
- **Searches:** text you search for from the box goes to your default search engine through Chrome, the same as searching from the address bar.

These requests go straight from your browser to those sites. They do not include your Forge Tab data.

## Permissions

- **Storage and unlimited storage:** to save your layout, notes and custom icons.
- **Favicon:** to show the icons Chrome already has for your shortcuts.
- **Search:** to use your default search engine.
- **Access to websites (optional):** only requested the first time you use Get logo or add an Ollama check, and only used for those features.
